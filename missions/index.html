Missions

Fulani Ministries exists to bring the Gospel to the Fulani people — the largest unreached people group in the world — while working practically to relieve poverty and promote education in the communities we serve.

Our Three-Fold Vision

  • To further the Christian faith among the Fulani people
  • To relieve poverty and suffering among the Fulani people
  • To promote education among the Fulani people

This vision is worked out through church planting and discipleship training, education at the Jam Tan Primary & Preschools, and relief work including business training, agriculture and accessible healthcare.

In Partnership

Fulani Ministries works in partnership with Elim Missions, who have supported founder Boureima Diallo as a missionary in Burkina Faso for many years, as well as with Compassion International for child sponsorship at the Jam Tan Centre.
