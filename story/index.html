Our Story

Fulani Ministries was born out of the vision God gave to a Fulani Christian, Boureima Diallo, in 1997 in Burkina Faso, West Africa.

Boureima was brought up as a Muslim in a large family and first heard about Jesus from a family friend, Adama, when he was thirteen years old. In 1985 he gave his life to Christ — a decision that brought significant opposition from his wider family and community, but which his own father, remarkably, encouraged.

Boureima went on to study Applied Theology and Cross-Cultural Studies at Moorlands College in the UK, and later pursued further theological study in London. In 1997, God called him to leave his studies and dedicate himself fully to reaching the Fulani people with the Gospel — a calling he fought against before finally saying yes.

A number of Fulani Christians became involved, working alongside Boureima, and Fulani Ministries grew into an interdenominational evangelism ministry now reaching Fulani communities in several countries of Sub-Saharan Africa. The ministry’s work is centred on the Jam Tan Centre in Burkina Faso, which today combines church planting, education and relief work for the surrounding community.
